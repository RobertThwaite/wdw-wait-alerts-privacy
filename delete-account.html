Delete your WDW Wait Alerts account

Last updated: 30 July 2026

You can ask us to delete your account and the data attached to it at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to make the request.

How to request deletion

Email crvibers@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email address on it
  • Your watchlist and per-ride alert settings
  • Your show and dining reminders
  • Your notification (device) tokens
  • The record of which park you last reported being in

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. The app’s ride wait-time history is aggregate data about the parks themselves, not about you, and is not linked to your account.
